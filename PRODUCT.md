# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Hosted on GitHub Pages because it is free, so the site must stay static files (no server runtime). The current build is plain HTML/CSS on Bootstrap 5.2. The user allows refactoring and redesign within that hosting constraint.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Iyad Mahdy for data science and AI engineering roles, and potential freelance or client contacts. Both skim quickly, want proof of shipped work, and the next step is to open the resume or send a message.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Iyad Mahdy, a data scientist and AI engineer. It shows working projects, experience and certificates, and makes it easy to read the resume or get in touch. Success is a recruiter or client reaching the resume or contact form after seeing real evidence.

## Positioning

An end-to-end builder of working AI systems: RAG and LLM pipelines that are deployed, with case studies, rather than notebooks only.

## Operating Context

Pages: Home, Projects, Resume, Contact, and three case studies (SmartCA, Kidic, Nexora). The contact form posts to Formspree. The resume is a Google Drive link. Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub or a resume link, often on a phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages; no backend of its own.
- Contact form via Formspree.
- Facts in the case studies (for example Nexora's "91 of 92 commits") must stay as written.
- Nexora has no demo video yet; SmartCA does.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Iyad Mahdy". Wording and case-study facts are to be preserved. No other visual commitment was made; the user explicitly allows redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case studies: `smartca-case-study.html`, `kidic-chatbot-case-study.html`, `nexora-case-study.html`.
- Other projects linked to GitHub and Kaggle (customer segmentation, A/B testing, diabetes prediction).
- Certificates (DataCamp, Credly, ALX) and a Drive resume, linked from the Resume page.
- A SmartCA demo video (Google Drive embed).
- Absent: testimonials, client logos and measured business results. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show real, linkable evidence before claims.
2. A hiring manager should reach the resume or contact within seconds.
3. Never fabricate testimonials, metrics or credentials.
4. Preserve the user's wording and verified facts.
5. Stay light and fast on GitHub Pages and on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement was stated. The site aims for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access (skip link, visible focus).
