Smart Contract Analyzer

Graduation Project · Team of 6

A full-stack AI system that reads Arabic employment contracts and checks every clause against Egyptian Labor Law 14/2025 — combining OCR, retrieval-augmented legal search, and an LLM compliance engine with a dedicated hallucination-grounding step, behind a FastAPI backend and a Flutter mobile/desktop/web client.

Python FastAPI Celery + Redis FAISS / RAG Groq LLM Azure Document Intelligence Flutter / Dart JWT Auth
Backend Repo (dev) Flutter App Repo

Team project — both repos are hosted under teammates' GitHub accounts as this was a shared academic build. My individual contributions are detailed below.

The problem

Employment contracts in Egypt are routinely signed without either side checking them against the current labor law — and the law itself changed in 2025, replacing the older 12/2003 statute most people still reference. SmartCA lets someone upload a contract as a photo or PDF and get back a clause-by-clause compliance read against Law 14/2025: which clauses are compliant, which are risky, and which specific law article each verdict is grounded in.

How an analysis flows through the system

Six stages run for every contract, from upload to a scored, citation-backed result back in the app.

  1. UploadContract PDF/photo uploaded from the Flutter app; a Celery task picks it up asynchronously.
  2. OCR + clause splitAzure Document Intelligence extracts Arabic text, which is normalized and parsed into individual clauses.
  3. Legal retrievalEach clause is matched against a 709-chunk indexed corpus of Law 14/2025 via FAISS + e5-base embeddings, then dual-gate filtered for relevance.
  4. LLM compliance checkA Groq-hosted LLM reasons over the clause and its retrieved articles to assign a low/medium/high risk verdict with a cited article.
  5. Grounding validatorAn anti-hallucination pass checks the LLM's claims are actually grounded in the cited law text before a verdict is accepted.
  6. Compliance scorePer-clause verdicts roll up into a compliance score and band, persisted to the DB and polled by the Flutter app.

My contribution

Built with a five-person team of AI and Flutter engineers; this section covers the parts I personally built, checked against the project's git history.

Backend & pipeline
Architecture
Architected the backend's async analysis pipeline connecting Celery/Redis task queues, Azure OCR, the FAISS/e5-base retriever, and the Groq LLM client, including the anti-hallucination grounding validator. Built role-based JWT authentication and the contract-management REST APIs (upload, listing, rename, original-file access). Wrote a one-command dev orchestration script (Redis + Celery worker + Flower + Uvicorn) and 10+ internal docs covering architecture, environment setup, and testing to onboard the rest of the team.
Precision tuning
LLM evaluation
Diagnosed why the compliance engine was over-flagging clauses: two legal-article citations in the rule engine were wrong (probation clause cited Article 47 instead of 90; working-hours clauses cited 137/138 instead of 117/121), traced by cross-checking the model's citations directly against the indexed law text. Fixed the mappings and shipped a deterministic precision guard that downgrades over-confident verdicts without touching legitimate ones. Measured against the team's labeled benchmark contracts, this raised violation-detection precision from 60% to 75% (F1 0.72 → 0.82) while holding recall exactly at 90%.
Flutter client
Feature work
Built the all-contracts list and rename screen, an in-app viewer for the original uploaded document, and the chunk-level legal-citation display on the analysis results screen — surfacing the exact law article behind each clause verdict directly in the UI.
